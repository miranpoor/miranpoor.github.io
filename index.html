How It Works

Everything happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

1

Set up once

Install Memoyad and let it go through your photos, screenshots and documents once. It runs on your phone and keeps up with new ones as they arrive.

2

Describe it

Type what you remember the way you would say it to a friend. No keywords, folders or tags needed.

3

Find it

Memoyad shows the best matches right away, like "me and dad laughing at the beach", or the parking receipt you need.
