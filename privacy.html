Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Applies to the Memoyad app for Android and the memoyad.com website

The short version: Memoyad analyzes your media with AI models that run on your phone. Your media, what the app learns about them, and your searches stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we have no servers that receive your media.

Information about you reaches us only when you choose to send it: a diagnostics report, an email, the contact form, or the beta waitlist. We never sell it.

1. Who we are

Memoyad is made by Tibaf Inc., a company based in Ontario, Canada ("Tibaf", "we", "us"). Tibaf Inc. is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Privacy Officer (the person in charge of the protection of personal information):
Privacy Officer, Tibaf Inc.
miranpour@tibaf.com

Write to the Privacy Officer with any question, request or complaint about your personal information.

2. What happens on your device

With your permission, Memoyad reads the images in your media library, analyzes them with on-device AI models, and stores a private search index (information about what each item shows, including any text in it) in the app's private storage on your phone. Your searches are run against that index on your phone.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is not included in Android cloud backups: Memoyad opts out of Google cloud backup, so the index is not copied to your Google account. If you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, the index may be copied directly to the new phone. The app does not make decisions about you; the AI only helps you find your own media.

3. Information that leaves your device, and when

AI model download The first time you use Memoyad, it downloads its AI model files (about 3.8 GB) from Hugging Face, a public model-hosting service. The request contains no information about you or your media, but like any download it reveals your device's IP address to the host, which handles it under Hugging Face's Privacy Policy. We do not receive it.
Diagnostics reports (optional) If you tap Settings > Send Diagnostics, or accept the offer to send a report after a crash, Memoyad creates a text report and hands it to the app you pick (for example, your email app), which sends it to us. The report contains your device model and Android version, the app's settings, counts and statistics about your library and its analysis, and the app's recent activity log, which may include text you searched for. It does not contain your media or what the app has stored about individual items. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and you can read the whole report first under Settings > View Diagnostics.
Emails and the contact form If you email us or use the contact form on memoyad.com, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and your message. Contact-form messages reach us through Formspree, a form-handling service.
Beta waitlist If you join the waitlist on memoyad.com, we receive the email address you enter, through Formspree.
Google Play Google Play gives us aggregated statistics (for example, install counts) and crash and performance reports (for example, crash stack traces, device model and Android version), under your Google Play and Android settings. These do not include your media or your searches. See Google's Privacy Policy.
Visiting memoyad.com The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website itself uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

4. How we use it, and why we are allowed to

  • Diagnostics reports: to find and fix the problem you are having and to improve Memoyad. We rely on your consent, which you give by choosing to send the report.
  • Emails and contact-form messages: to answer you and to provide support. We rely on your consent and on our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • Waitlist email: only to send you Memoyad beta invitations and launch news from Tibaf Inc. We rely on your consent. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • Google Play statistics and crash reports: to keep the app stable and fix crashes, based on our legitimate interest in providing a working app.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not combine it with other information to profile you.

5. Who else handles it

We share personal information only with the service providers that handle it for us, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Formspree (United States): receives waitlist sign-ups and contact-form messages. Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Our email provider: stores the emails and reports you send us.
  • Google (Google Play Console): provides the app statistics and crash reports described above.
  • GitHub (United States): hosts the website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order, or to protect the safety of any person. If Tibaf Inc. or the Memoyad app is ever sold or merged, personal information may pass to the new owner, who must keep protecting it under this policy.

6. Where it is stored

We are in Canada, and several of our service providers store data in the United States or other countries. While there, your information is subject to the laws of those countries and may be accessible to their courts, law enforcement and national security authorities. Where the law requires it (for example, for people in the European Union or United Kingdom), these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses used by our providers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Diagnostics reports, emails and contact-form messages: until the matter is resolved, and no longer than 12 months after our last exchange with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Waitlist email addresses: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you, and no longer than 12 months after Memoyad's public launch.
  • Google Play and GitHub data: kept by those companies under their own retention policies.

8. How we protect it

Everything Memoyad keeps on your phone stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Information you send us travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The accounts where we receive it are limited to the people who need them and are protected with strong authentication, including two-factor authentication. We delete information when the retention periods above end. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; if a breach ever affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before you withdrew it). To use any of these rights, write to the Privacy Officer at miranpour@tibaf.com. We will answer within 30 days, and we may ask you to confirm your identity first. It is free.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to a privacy regulator:

  • In Canada: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
  • In Quebec: the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
  • In the European Union: your country's data protection authority. In the United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office.

10. Permissions Memoyad asks for

  • Photos and media: to analyze your media and search them. This is the app's core function.
  • Notifications: to show download and analysis progress.
  • Internet: to download the AI models. Analysis and search work offline.
  • Running in the background and battery optimization: so analysis of a large library can finish while you are not using the app. You can decline.

11. Your choices and deleting your data

Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your data page.

  • You can revoke media access at any time in Android Settings.
  • To delete everything Memoyad stored on your phone, uninstall the app or use App info > Storage > Clear storage.
  • To unsubscribe from waitlist emails, use the link in any email or write to us.
  • To have us delete a diagnostics report, email, contact-form message or waitlist entry, write to the Privacy Officer.

12. Children

Memoyad and memoyad.com are intended for adults and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly receive personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact the Privacy Officer and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also say so in the app's release notes, and we will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
