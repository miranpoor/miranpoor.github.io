Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · Applies to the Memoyad app for Android and the memoyad.com website

The short version: Memoyad analyzes your media with AI models that run on your phone. Your media, what the app learns about them, and your searches stay on your device. We have no servers that receive them, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

The only time information about you reaches us is when you choose to send us a diagnostics report or an email.

Who we are

Memoyad is developed by Tibaf Inc ("we", "us"). You can reach us at miranpour@tibaf.com.

What happens on your device

With your permission, Memoyad reads the images in your media library, analyzes them with on-device AI models, and stores a private search index (information about what each item shows, including any text in it) in the app's private storage on your phone. Your searches are run against that index on your phone.

None of this is uploaded. It is not included in Android cloud backups: Memoyad opts out of Google cloud backup, so the index is not copied to your Google account. If you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, the index may be copied directly to the new phone.

What leaves your device, and when

AI model download The first time you use Memoyad, it downloads its AI model files (about 3.8 GB) from Hugging Face, a public model-hosting service. The request contains no information about you or your media. As with any download, the hosting service sees your device's IP address; see Hugging Face's Privacy Policy.
Diagnostics reports (optional) If you tap Settings > Send Diagnostics, or accept the offer to send a report after a crash, Memoyad creates a text report and hands it to the app you pick (for example, your email app), which sends it to us. The report contains your device model and Android version, the app's settings, counts and statistics about your library and its analysis, and the app's recent activity log, which may include text you searched for. It does not contain your media or what the app has stored about individual items. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
Emails you send us If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.
Google Play Google Play may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports (for example, crash stack traces and device model) under your Google Play and Android settings. These do not include your media or searches.

How we use what we receive

We use diagnostics reports, Play crash reports and emails only to fix problems, improve Memoyad, and answer you. We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. We keep reports only as long as we need them to resolve the issue, and no longer than 12 months.

Permissions Memoyad asks for

  • Photos and media: to analyze your media and search them. This is the app's core function.
  • Notifications: to show analysis progress.
  • Internet: to download the AI models. Analysis and search work offline.
  • Running in the background and battery optimization: so analysis of a large library can finish while you're not using the app. You can decline.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can revoke media access at any time in Android Settings.
  • To delete everything Memoyad stored on your phone, uninstall the app or use App info > Storage > Clear storage.
  • To have us delete a diagnostics report or email you sent, write to us at the address above.

The website

If you join the beta waitlist on memoyad.com, the email address you enter is collected through our form provider, Formspree, and is used only to tell you about Memoyad's release. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

If you use the contact form on memoyad.com, we receive the name, email address and message you enter, also through Formspree, and use them only to reply to you.

Children

Memoyad is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will say so in the app's release notes.
